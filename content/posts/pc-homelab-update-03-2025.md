## PC upgrade

The MINISFORUM mobo is going to be an interesting one to build.
It is Mini ITX small form factor, which is something that I have been wanting
to do for quite some time now.

The mobo actually has a heatsink/cpu onboard which is quite nice. It has 2
SODIMM slots and PCIe 5.0. My intentions are to replace my current desktop.

I have the DIMMs and Power Supply ready to go.
I just need to pick out a Mini ITX case and it will be time to assemble!!
Don't worry though, when that time comes, there will be another post.

I am on the fence on whether I will be upgrading my GPU. It hasn't exactly been
a great few years innovation and delivery when it comes to GPUs and that leads
me to the thought that sourcing a graphics card which is *reasonably priced*
will absolutely be the hardest part of this upgrade.

I am currently running a AMD 5700XT, which has served well and continues to
run like a champ for most games at a more than decent frame-rate.
I do have my eyes on something like a
RTX 4080 or recently announced [RX 9070](https://www.techpowerup.com/gpu-specs/radeon-rx-9070-xt.c4229).

I'm also pretty content with my 5700XT for the time being and
unless I find a decent enough sale, I will probably coast it out for the
next couple months.
