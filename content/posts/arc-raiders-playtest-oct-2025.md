This weekend, I have been entrenched in the
[Arc Raiders](https://arcraiders.com/en/) "Server Slam" playtest.

![img](https://static0.gamerantimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/arc-raiders-frequently-asked-questions.jpg?q=49&fit=crop&w=825&dpr=2)

After sinking some hours in **Arc Raiders** this weekend with some friends, I can
see the why this genre of game (extraction shooter) are so good / addictive.

Here's some initial thoughts:

**Gameplay**

The looting mechanics, shooting, upgrading and crafting are really satisfying.
The guns are really unique and feel balanced. If you have to use the "free
loadout" due to not having any materials to craft or buy a weapon - it does not
feel like a huge deal. Each decision to stay or continue looting feels like it
should -- a gamble to get more loot vs returning safely to the elevator with
what's in your backpack.

**Sound**

The sound design is awesome and complements the gameplay. Everything is punchy
and over-the-top. There is also proximity chat which is a must for this type
of game.

**Graphics**

This game is one of the best Unreal Engine 5 titles I have seen so far. The
environments, lighting, character models and everything in between look
so good.

**UI**

The UI for crafting is a bit buried in the menus.
Recycling materials also feels like a chore - I would
often find myself wanting an "auto-recycle" button
to get the components required to upgrade and craft gear.

**Stability**

I should also say the game was stable for me on Linux (using Proton
Experimental) on Steam. Latency with the game servers was minimal
for me. Both of these were not expected, but very nice nonetheless.

**Overall**

It feels like a really good blend of recent shooters - Helldivers 2, Apex
Legends, Escape from Tarkov, Battlefront II and others.

I am patiently waiting for the end of this month to be able to play the full game.
